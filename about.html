About Us

Procastinaut is a webpage designed to increase your productiviy!
You can add tasks to your to-do list, structure your workflows using the Pomodoro timer, manage events in your calendar, and take notes in your notepad. We also have some un background customisation options so you can personalise the look of your workspace! !
